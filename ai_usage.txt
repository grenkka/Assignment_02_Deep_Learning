Huge weights make the model "afraid" of missing rare classes, leading to a massive amount of False Positives. To fix this, you need to strictly limit the maximum weight value and slightly decrease the learning rate for stability. Add np.clip to your calculation:

pos_counts = train_df[LABEL_COLS].sum().values
neg_counts = len(train_df) - pos_counts
pos_weights = neg_counts / np.maximum(pos_counts, 1)
pos_weights = np.clip(pos_weights, a_min=1.0, a_max=10.0) # Limit
pos_weights_tensor = torch.tensor(pos_weights, dtype=torch.float32).to(DEVICE)
